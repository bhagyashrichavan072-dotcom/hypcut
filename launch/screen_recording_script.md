# HypeCut demo: screen-recording script

You only record your screen and say the lines. Text, zooms, music and graphics get added in the edit afterwards.

**Record with:** Win+Alt+R (Xbox Game Bar) or OBS, full screen, 1080p or higher. Mic on.
**Before recording:**
- Close every window except the HypeCut app.
- Have one stream link copied, from a stream you're allowed to clip.
- Do one practice run first so **My clips** already has 6 clips. You'll cut the waiting part anyway.
- Don't open the Settings tab with your real key on screen. For the key part, record the Settings tab with the key field empty.

---

## Take 1: the full walkthrough (record in one go, ~2–3 min raw)

| Step | What you do on screen | What you say (Hinglish) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Desktop → double-click the **HypeCut** icon. App opens. | "Ye hai HypeCut. Free hai, GitHub pe hai, aur aapke laptop pe chalta hai." |
| 2 | Hover over the 4 tiles at the top. | "Upar dikhta hai kitne clips bane, best viral score, aur aaj ke credits." |
| 3 | Click **Video link**. Paste the stream link. | "Kisi bhi finished Twitch ya YouTube stream ka link paste karo." |
| 4 | Click **Clip options**, show *Clips per run = 6*, close it. | "Har run mein 6 alag clips banta hai, 6 alag moments se." |
| 5 | Click **Start clipping**. | "Start dabao." |
| 6 | Point the mouse at the **Runs** box as the log scrolls. | "Ab HypeCut poora chat padh raha hai. Jahan chat explode hota hai, KEKW, CLIP IT, wahi moment pakadta hai. Phir Whisper best part chunta hai." |
| 7 | (Cut the wait.) Click **My clips**. Scroll the 6 clips. | "Aur ye rahe 6 clips. Vertical, captions ke saath, ready to post." |
| 8 | Play one clip for 5–6 s. | "Hook, title, word-by-word captions, zoom: sab automatic." |
| 9 | Point at the 🔥 viral score, then click **Copy caption + hashtags**. | "Har clip ka viral score, aur caption plus hashtags ek click mein copy." |
| 10 | Click **Download**. | "Download karo aur post karo." |
| 11 | Click **⚙ Settings** (key field empty). Point at the Gemini key box. | "Bas ek free Gemini API key chahiye. aistudio.google.com se free mein milti hai. Yahan paste karo, save karo." |
| 12 | Click **★ Founder**. | "HypeCut maine banaya hai. Kunal, JR Revenue Systems." |
| 13 | Back to **⚡ Clip**. | "Comment karo CLIP, main GitHub link DM kar dunga." |

## Take 2: install with Claude Code (optional, ~30 s raw)
| Step | What you do on screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open Claude Code and paste the install prompt from the README. | "Install karna bhi easy hai. Claude Code mein ye ek prompt paste karo." |
| 2 | Let it run (cut the wait), end on the HypeCut icon on the Desktop. | "Aur HypeCut aapke desktop pe ready." |

## Extra screen B-roll (5–10 s each)
- The desktop icon being double-clicked
- Typing / pasting the link
- The Runs log scrolling
- Scrolling through My clips
- One clip playing full screen (open the MP4 from Downloads)
- The credits tile and the viral score badge

## Keep out of the recording
- Your real Gemini key
- Pricing, Pro, limits (these come after launch)
- Footage of streamers who didn't give permission (use your own stream or the **Demo** run)
